Phase.Mirror

Two independent quaternion fields occupy each half of the screen. Your mouse position on either side sets that side's quaternion via axis-angle mapping. Each quaternion drives two oscillators whose frequencies are derived from the quaternion's Euler angle decomposition. The central line represents the 'commutator' — the mathematical difference between q_left × q_right and q_right × q_left. When both sides converge to similar rotations, the commutator approaches zero and a pure harmonic tone emerges. Maximum divergence produces maximum dissonance.
