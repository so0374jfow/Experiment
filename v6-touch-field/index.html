Touch.Field

Sound is generated by mapping your touch position to a quaternion rotation. The X-axis selects the rotation axis in 3D space (i, j, or k), while Y controls the rotation angle. The resulting quaternion components (w, x, y, z) directly set oscillator frequencies. Distance from screen center determines the fundamental pitch — center is low (55 Hz), edges are high (880 Hz). Vertical position sweeps a low-pass filter from 200 Hz to 8000 Hz. The point cloud rotates by the same quaternion that drives the sound, making the math visible.
