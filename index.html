01 — QUAT.FIELD
Ultra-minimal microsound. A grid of points rotating via quaternion SLERP. Sparse sine bursts, sub-bass drones, architectural precision. Sound as spatial geometry.
After Alva Noto
02 — EIGENSPACE
Psychoacoustic FM synthesis driven by non-commutative quaternion products. q1·q2 ≠ q2·q1 becomes audible spectral asymmetry. Beating frequencies, Shepard tones, perceptual instability.
After Florian Hecker
03 — HAMILTON'S BRIDGE
The quaternion algebra itself is the composition. i, j, k are oscillators in just intonation. When i·j=k, harmonic reinforcement. When i²=−1, octave descent. Hopf fibration visualization.
After William Rowan Hamilton
04 — ROTORSOUND
Kinetic sound sculpture. Orbiting rings with irrational period ratios (π, e, √2, φ) create non-repeating polyrhythm. Proximity triggers percussive tones. Quaternion rotation as rhythm machine.
After Zimoun / Ryoji Ikeda
05 — MANIFOLD
Immersive drone continuum. Quaternion walkers on S³ trace paths via stereographic projection. Particle clouds coalesce and dissolve. Layered harmonics over 30–120 second arcs. Rotation space is continuous.
After Éliane Radigue
