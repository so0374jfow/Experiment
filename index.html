Quaternion Sound Synthesis

q = w + xi + yj + zk
Fifteen experiments exploring how quaternion
imaginary numbers produce sound, space, and movement.
Generative — Non-Interactive
01 — QUAT.FIELD
Minimal microsound. Point grid rotating via SLERP. Sparse sine bursts, sub-bass drones.
After Alva Noto
02 — EIGENSPACE
Psychoacoustic FM synthesis. Non-commutative quaternion products create spectral asymmetry.
After Florian Hecker
03 — HAMILTON'S BRIDGE
Algebra as music. i, j, k are oscillators in just intonation. Hopf fibration visualization.
After William Rowan Hamilton
04 — ROTORSOUND
Kinetic sculpture. Orbiting rings with irrational period ratios create polyrhythm.
After Zimoun / Ryoji Ikeda
05 — MANIFOLD
Drone continuum. Quaternion walkers on S3 via stereographic projection. Particle clouds.
After Eliane Radigue
Interactive — Touch / Mouse
06 — TOUCH.FIELD
Touch position drives quaternion rotation of a point cloud. Distance from center sets pitch. High-frequency stutter oscillators respond to gesture velocity.
Interactive — Drag to play
07 — SPIN.LATTICE
Drag to spin a wireframe lattice. Angular velocity maps to pitch and stutter density. Each rotation axis activates a different oscillator bank.
Interactive — Drag to spin
08 — CHORD.SPACE
Place nodes on a sphere. Angular distance between quaternions determines musical intervals. Close nodes produce consonance, distant nodes dissonance.
Interactive — Click to place nodes
09 — WAVE.COLLAPSE
Click to spawn circular waves. Overlapping waves multiply quaternions creating FM interference. Rapid clicks produce dense stutter textures.
Interactive — Click to spawn waves
10 — AXIS.LOCK
Toggle i, j, k axes on and off. Each axis drives its own oscillator. Mouse height controls rotation speed and stutter rate.
Interactive — Toggle axes
11 — PHASE.MIRROR
Split screen. Each side has its own quaternion field. Convergence produces harmony, divergence produces dissonance. Center line is the commutator.
Interactive — Move on each side
12 — GRAIN.CLOUD
Granular synthesis in quaternion space. Mouse spawns 20-80ms sine grains. Speed controls density. High-pitch micro-grains stutter at gesture peaks.
Interactive — Move to scatter grains
13 — STRING.TENSOR
Eight strings tuned to quaternion-derived harmonics. Pluck by dragging. Neighboring strings resonate via quaternion coupling.
Interactive — Drag to pluck
14 — PULSE.GRID
8x8 step sequencer. Cell position maps to quaternion pitch and rhythm. Toggle cells to compose. Stutter patterns from high-speed column triggers.
Interactive — Click cells
15 — ORBIT.DRAW
Draw paths that become quaternion orbits via SQUAD interpolation. Dots travel your curves generating sound. Multiple orbits for polyphony.
Interactive — Draw orbits
