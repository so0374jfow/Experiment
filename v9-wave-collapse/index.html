Wave.Collapse

Each click spawns a circular wave with a random quaternion identity. As waves expand and overlap, their quaternions are multiplied together — and because quaternion multiplication is non-commutative (q1×q2 ≠ q2×q1), the order of overlap matters. The product quaternion's components modulate FM synthesis parameters: the w component sets the carrier frequency (110–880 Hz), x sets the modulation ratio, and y×z sets the modulation depth. Where many waves intersect, the cascading quaternion products create complex, evolving timbres.
