Axis.Lock

The three imaginary components of a quaternion — i, j, k — are the building blocks of 3D rotation. Here each component drives its own oscillator: i produces a sine wave at 264 Hz (C4), j a sine at 330 Hz (E4), k a sine at 396 Hz (G4) — forming a just-intonation major triad. By toggling axes, you hear how each dimension of rotation contributes to the full harmonic structure. The continuously rotating quaternion modulates each oscillator's amplitude — when a component is near zero, its tone fades; when near ±1, it's loudest. Mouse height controls rotation speed.
