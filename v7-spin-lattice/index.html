Spin.Lattice

You spin a 3D lattice by clicking and dragging. Your drag gesture is converted to a quaternion rotation using the axis-angle method — the drag direction becomes the rotation axis, drag speed becomes the angle. The quaternion's three imaginary components (x, y, z) each drive a separate oscillator: x controls a sine wave, y a triangle wave, z a filtered sawtooth. Angular velocity maps to pitch (0–1200 Hz) and amplitude. The lattice has physical inertia — it decelerates via quaternion SLERP toward identity, and the sound fades with it.
