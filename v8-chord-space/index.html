Chord.Space

Each point you place on the sphere represents a unit quaternion — a point in 4D rotation space projected onto 3D. The angular distance between any two quaternions (measured by the .angleTo() method) determines their musical interval. Close quaternions (small angle) produce consonant intervals like octaves and fifths. Distant quaternions (angle near π) produce dissonant intervals like tritones. Each node generates a sine tone whose frequency is derived from its quaternion components via: freq = 220 × (1 + |q.x| + |q.y| × 0.5 + |q.z| × 0.25).
